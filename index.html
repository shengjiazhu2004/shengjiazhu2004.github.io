Research Project

VOKE: Visual Object Kinematics Estimator for Dexterous In-Hand Manipulation

Shengjia Zhu, Wenqiang Xu, Lin Shao

Manuscript under review, 2026

We propose a framework to recover an object's full kinematic state from hand-object interaction videos, and then use these estimates to refine frame-wise object poses and provide kinematic states for dexterous in-hand manipulation.
